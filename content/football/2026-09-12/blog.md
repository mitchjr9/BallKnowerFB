## A note on early-season confidence

14 of these games involve a team with almost no football on the books this season, so the ratings still lean on last year's results carried forward and regressed toward the mean rather than on anything that has happened yet. The model knows this and caps how confident it's allowed to be. Ratings firm up considerably by early October.

## How to read this

Every number here is a probability, not a prediction. A 70% pick is *supposed* to lose three times in ten — if it never did, the number would be wrong. What matters over a season isn't the hit rate, it's whether the 70s land near 70. Every forecast is timestamped and locked before kickoff, and graded afterward whether it worked or not.
